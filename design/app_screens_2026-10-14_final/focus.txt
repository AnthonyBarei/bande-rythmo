## Fixes
- index.css: :focus-visible ring is !important (beats inline box-shadows of the primitives).
- index.css: label:has(> input:focus-visible) draws the ring for the opacity-0 colour input (Réglages, custom accent).
- index.css + .focus-inset: inset 2px accent ring for flush controls inside overflow-clipped strips (library filter tabs,
  card action cells, Studio take cards), whose outer ring was cut on 2–3 sides.
- ui.jsx pressable(): role=button + tabIndex 0 + Enter/Space → click, used by Chip/Card (when clickable), Toggle
  (role=switch, aria-label), ImportSection drop zone, ClipCard rename, InspectorBoucles / InspectorVoix rows,
  RecorderPanel take card, meme layer rows, LexiconPanel phonetic edit. Verified: Space flips Réglages and Exporter toggles.

## Known limitation
- Library card action row (Doubler / mic / MP3 / GIF / trash) is visibility:hidden until the card gets focus-within;
  React reveals it on the focus event, so Tab presses closer than ~100 ms apart skip it (normal typing speed reaches it).
